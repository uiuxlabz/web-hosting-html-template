FAQ

Quick answers to common questions

Before reaching out, see if your question is answered below.

How do I migrate my existing website to GreenHost?

We offer free automated migration for all shared hosting plans. Our support team handles the entire transfer — just provide your current hosting credentials and we take care of the rest. Most migrations complete within 24 hours with zero downtime.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and cryptocurrency payments. Every plan is backed by a 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade from any plan at any time. We'll prorate the difference and your site stays live throughout the process with no downtime. If you outgrow shared hosting, moving to VPS or dedicated takes just a few clicks.

Do you really run on 100% renewable energy?

Yes. GreenHost partners with renewable energy providers and purchases carbon offsets equivalent to 150% of our energy consumption. We also plant trees for every hosting plan sold — over 41,000 trees planted to date.
