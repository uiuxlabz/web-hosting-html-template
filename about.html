About GreenHost

We're on a mission to make every website on the internet run on clean, renewable energy.

Our Story

Born from a simple green idea

GreenHost was founded in 2019 by a small team of developers and environmental scientists who were tired of watching the web's carbon footprint grow. We believed hosting could be fast, affordable — and kind to the planet.

Today we power more than 24,000 websites from energy-efficient data centers across three continents. Every server runs on 100% renewable energy, and every customer directly contributes to reforestation projects around the world.

Our Values

What we stand for

Three principles guide every decision we make — from server hardware to how we support you.

Sustainability

We power every byte with renewables, build energy-efficient hardware, and plant a tree for every new customer.

Performance

Green doesn't mean slow. Our LiteSpeed stack and NVMe storage rank among the fastest in the industry.

Transparency

Clear pricing, no hidden fees, and public reporting on our energy usage and reforestation impact.
