FAQ

Hosting questions, answered

How is GreenHost actually green?

Our data centers are powered by 100% renewable energy through wind and solar PPAs, verified by renewable energy certificates. We also plant a tree for every new customer and run high-efficiency, e-waste-free hardware.

Can I migrate my existing website?

Yes! Our team offers free website migration. Just open a support ticket after ordering and we'll move your site, databases and email over — usually within 24 hours and with zero downtime.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Every shared plan comes with a 30-day, no-questions-asked money-back guarantee. VPS and dedicated plans include a 14-day guarantee.

What if my website grows beyond my plan?

You can upgrade to a higher plan at any time in your control panel. Upgrades are prorated instantly — you only pay the difference, and there's never any downtime.
